Zero-Install Browser Flasher

🏰 MSEP Costume Firmware Installer

Flash the latest compiled 90-second show sequence and ESP-NOW fleet sync firmware directly from your web browser! No Arduino IDE, Python, or command line needed.

  1. Connect your ESP32 to your computer using a USB data cable.
  2. Click the green "Connect & Flash ESP32" button below.
  3. In the Chrome/Edge pop-up, select your ESP32's serial port (e.g. CP2102, CH340, or USB Serial) and click Connect.
  4. Click Install and watch the progress bar reach 100%!

💡 Tip: If it fails to connect, hold down the onboard BOOT button on your ESP32 for 2 seconds while connecting!

⚠️ Web Serial is not supported in this browser. Please open this page in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge!
Target: ESP32 DevKit · Output: GPIO 16 (WS2812B) · Toggle: BOOT Pin (GPIO 0)
