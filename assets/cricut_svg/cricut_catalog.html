💡 Cricut Cutting & Heat Press Tips for 10K Running Shirts

  • Chest Placement Clearance: Keep the graphic scaled to approx 7.0" to 7.5" wide. Place it between 2" below the collar and at least 1" above the race bib (default 57% torso height) to ensure BibBoards fasteners don't clamp over vinyl.
  • Vinyl Type Recommendation: Use SportFlex Iron-On or Siser EasyWeed Stretch for stretchy technical shirts. For the white/gold layer, consider Glow-in-the-Dark HTV for extra magic during the early 5:00 AM race start!
  • Mirror Before Cutting: Always toggle "Mirror (On)" in Cricut Design Space when cutting Iron-On / Heat Transfer Vinyl.
  • Pressing Sequence: Press the bottom/base layer first for only 3-5 seconds (tack press), peel warm, then align the next layer. Finish with a full 15-second press on the final layer with a Teflon or parchment protective sheet.
