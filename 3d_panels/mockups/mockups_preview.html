🖨️ Slicer Instructions for Accurate Weight:

  • Infill Density: 100% (or set Top/Bottom Shell Layers to 99). Do not use 15% infill or it will be 75% too light!

  • Print Time: ~45 min for 100mm tile; ~1.5 hours for 200mm plate.
