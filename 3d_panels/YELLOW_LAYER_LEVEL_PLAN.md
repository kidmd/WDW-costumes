## 2. Root Cause Analysis: Why Did Print 1 Come Out 0.2mm Off?

If the STL vertices are at the exact same $Z = 9.0000$, why did Print 1 slice/print as if it were 0.2mm off the bed? Three factors explain this behavior:

### A. Yellow Pigment Translucency & First-Layer Slicer Sensitivity
- **Yellow has the lowest pigment density of any FDM filament.** While green, magenta, and white achieve full opacity in 2–3 layers ($0.4\text{–}0.6\text{ mm}$), yellow is naturally semi-translucent.
- When printed over a black chassis or dark build plate, if yellow receives even one fewer layer, the dark background aggressively bleeds through.

### B. Bambu Studio Slicer First-Layer Slicing Dynamics
- **First Layer Height vs Standard Layer Height:** 
  - In Bambu Studio / OrcaSlicer, the default first layer height is often **$0.28\text{ mm}$** (or $0.20\text{ mm}$), with subsequent layers at **$0.20\text{ mm}$**.
  - Total inlay thickness is **$0.60\text{ mm}$**.
  - With a $0.28\text{ mm}$ first layer:
    - Layer 1: $0.00 \to 0.28\text{ mm}$ (Yellow)
    - Layer 2: $0.28 \to 0.48\text{ mm}$ (Yellow)
    - Layer 3: $0.48 \to 0.68\text{ mm}$ (Already past the $0.60\text{ mm}$ pocket ceiling! The slicer starts infilling black chassis filament at $0.60\text{ mm}$!).
  - Result: Only **two thin layers** of yellow were printed before solid black was laid over it!

### C. Elephant Foot Compensation or Auto-Drop Offset
- Bambu Studio includes an **Elephant Foot Compensation** setting ($0.15\text{–}0.20\text{ mm}$) and auto-arranging logic.
- If the yellow belly (which contains 25 separate disconnected polygon islands between the black rib stripes) was treated by the slicer as having a non-planar boundary or if one island didn't register as coplanar with the first layer, the slicer may have bridged layer 1 and started extrusion on layer 2.

## 3. Options to Permanently Fix the Model

We want the model to print **100% reliably on the first attempt without requiring manual slicer workarounds**:

### Option 1: Increase Inlay Thickness from 0.6mm to 0.8mm or 1.0mm (Recommended)
- **Change:** Increase `COLOR_INLAY_THICK` from $0.60\text{ mm}$ (3 layers) to **$0.80\text{ mm}$ (4 solid layers)** or **$1.00\text{ mm}$ (5 solid layers)**.
- **Why this works:**
  - Guarantees 4 to 5 full solid layers of colored filament before any black chassis floor is printed behind it.
  - Completely eliminates translucency for yellow (and all other colors), giving every print the rich, saturated appearance of the right print.
  - Leaves $1.0\text{–}1.2\text{ mm}$ of solid black chassis floor behind the graphic, maintaining full structural rigidity.

### Option 2: Add 0.15mm First-Layer "Bite" (Slight Proud Face)
- **Change:** Allow the inlays to extend $0.15\text{–}0.20\text{ mm}$ proud ($Z = 9.20\text{ mm}$ vs $9.00\text{ mm}$ for the chassis border).
- **Why this works:** Guarantees the inlays are the absolute first thing to touch the build plate when flipped face down, ensuring maximum bed squish and eliminating any chance of skipping Layer 1.
