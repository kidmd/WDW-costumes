# 🎨 Cricut Heat Transfer Vinyl (HTV) Costume Artwork Guide
### Vector Design Suite for Main Street Electrical Parade (WDW 10K Costumes)

This guide details the complete vector artwork suite designed specifically for cutting with a **Cricut** (Maker, Explore, or Joy Xtra) or **Silhouette** vinyl cutter.

All designs are clean, multi-layered SVG cut files with zero microscopic islands, ensuring easy weeding and durable adhesion on stretchy, moisture-wicking technical running shirts.

## 📏 Torso Placement & runDisney 10K Bib Clearance

Each design has been calibrated to fit the athletic chest zone without colliding with the neckline or the race bib:

* **Target Graphic Width:** **7.0" to 7.5" wide** (scales proportionally to ~5.2" to 5.5" high).
* **Vertical Torso Zone:** Place the top of the graphic **2.0" to 2.5" below the center crew neckline dip**.
* **Race Bib Clearance:** The bottom edge of the vinyl graphic sits **at least 1.0" to 1.5" above the race bib** (default 57% bib elevation), ensuring that the 4-corner **BibBoards snap fasteners** never puncture or clamp onto your vinyl artwork!

## ✂️ Step-by-Step Cricut Design Space Cutting Instructions

1. **Upload SVG to Cricut Design Space:**
   - Click **New Project** ➔ **Upload** ➔ **Upload Image**.
   - Browse and select your float's SVG file (e.g., `casey_jr_train.svg`).
   - Click **Upload** and then **Add to Canvas**.
2. **Set the Dimensions:**
   - In the top toolbar, ensure the **Lock Aspect Ratio** icon (🔒) is locked.
   - Enter **Width: 7.2"** (the height will automatically scale to ~5.4").
3. **Verify Mat Colors:**
   - In the right-hand **Layers panel**, you will see each color grouped cleanly.
   - You can toggle layers on or off if you want to inspect them individually.
4. **Click "Make It":**
   - Cricut Design Space will automatically separate your design onto 3 or 4 colored mats.
5. **CRITICAL: Mirror Every Mat:**
   - On the left mat preview screen, **turn the "Mirror" toggle ON for EVERY mat!**
   - *(Because HTV is cut from the back/adhesive side through the clear carrier sheet, all Iron-On must be mirrored).*
6. **Material Selection:**
   - Select **"Everyday Iron-On"** or **"SportFlex Iron-On"** (if using stretchy polyester athletic shirts).
   - Place your vinyl on the green StandardGrip mat **SHINY SIDE (Carrier Sheet) DOWN**.
7. **Weed the Excess Vinyl:**
   - Use your Cricut weeding tool to gently pull away the excess vinyl, leaving only your design on the sticky clear carrier sheet.

## 🔥 Multi-Layer Heat Press / Ironing Instructions

When pressing multiple layers of HTV onto technical athletic shirts, use the **"Tack & Press" method** to prevent fabric shrinkage or vinyl distortion:

* **Heat Press Temperature:** **305°F (150°C)** for standard HTV, or **280°F (140°C)** for SportFlex / Stretch HTV.
* **Pre-Press Fabric:** Press the blank shirt for 5 seconds to remove moisture and wrinkles.
* **Alignment:** Place the shirt flat, center the design 2" below the neckline.
* **Pressing Sequence:**
  1. **Layer 1 (Bottom Base Layer):** Place on shirt. Cover with a Teflon sheet or parchment paper. **Press for only 3 to 5 seconds** (just enough to "tack" it down). Peel the carrier sheet **warm**.
  2. **Layer 2 (Middle Accents):** Align the second carrier sheet over the first layer. Cover with parchment paper. **Press for 3 to 5 seconds**. Peel carrier sheet.
  3. **Layer 3 & 4 (Top Accents & White Highlights):** Align each remaining layer, tack for 3-5 seconds.
  4. **Final Cure Press:** Once all carrier sheets are removed, cover the entire completed graphic with a fresh parchment/Teflon sheet and **press firmly for a full 15 seconds** to permanently cure all layers into the fabric weave.
* **Post-Cure Care:** Allow 24 hours before wearing or washing. Wash inside-out in cold water; hang dry.

## 💡 Attaching WS2812B "Seed / Pebble" LEDs Over Vinyl

Once the vinyl is pressed, use the printed bulb dots, spokes, and contour lines on the artwork as precise physical anchor guides:

1. **Start Position:** LED 0 begins at the lower-left waist/hem (where the battery pack & ESP32 sit in your pocket).
2. **Anchor Points:** Use clear nylon thread (invisible monofilament) or small dabs of *Aleene's "OK to Wash It"* fabric adhesive to tack the discreet pebble LED nodes directly onto the vinyl light dots and contour lines.
3. **Bulb Spacing:** Seed/pebble LEDs are spaced at ~1.5" to 2" intervals, which neatly traces the curves of each float without pulling or bunching the shirt fabric!
