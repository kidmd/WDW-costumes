# ✂️ Cricut Master SVG Cut Guide for MSEP Mesh Pinnie Costumes
### Step-by-Step Instructions for Cricut Design Space (Brother's Quick-Start Guide)

This guide walks through exactly how to take the exported **Master SVG file** (e.g., `petes_dragon_cricut_full_artwork_with_6x3mm_slots.svg` or any float's cut file) and cut it on a Cricut machine with pre-aligned $6.0\,\text{mm} \times 3.0\,\text{mm}$ LED pill holes.

---

## 🛠️ What You Need
1. **Cricut Machine:** Cricut Explore (3 / Air 2) or Cricut Maker (3 / original).
2. **Cricut Design Space:** Official software on Mac or PC (free).
3. **Materials:**
   - **Heat Transfer Vinyl (HTV / Iron-On):** 
     - Mat 1: Emerald Green (Body)
     - Mat 2: Hot Pink / Magenta (Hair, Wings, & Spines)
     - *(Optional White/Orange for details depending on float)*
   - **Cricut StandardGrip Mat** (Green mat).
   - **Weeding Hook / Tweezers**.
   - **Heat Press / Home Iron / Cricut EasyPress** + Parchment Paper or Teflon Sheet.
   - **Mesh Pinnie Running Vest**.

---

## 📋 Step-by-Step Cricut Workflow

### Step 1: Upload the SVG File
1. Open **Cricut Design Space** on your computer and start a **New Project**.
2. Click the **Upload** button on the left sidebar, then click **Upload Image**.
3. Drag & drop the downloaded `petes_dragon_cricut_full_artwork_with_6x3mm_slots.svg` file (or click Browse to locate it).
4. Click **Upload** to save it to your library, select the thumbnail, and click **Add to Canvas**.

---

### Step 2: Set the Final Chest Width
1. With the design selected on the canvas, check the top toolbar for **Width (W)** and **Height (H)**.
2. Ensure the padlock icon between Width and Height is **LOCKED** (keeps the artwork proportional).
3. Set the **Width to ~7.2 inches** (or whatever chest width fits the mesh vest). Cricut will automatically adjust the height proportionally (~10.2 inches), and all 100 pill slots will scale in exact alignment.

---

### Step 3: Organize & "Attach" the Cutouts (The Most Important Step!)
When the SVG loads, Cricut sees distinct layers in the **Layers Panel** on the right:
- **`Layer_1_Green_Vinyl`:** Green Body & Legs
- **`Layer_2_Pink_Vinyl`:** Pink Hair Crest, Wings & Spines
- **`Layer_LED_Pill_Slots_6x3mm`:** The 100 Pill Slots ($6\times 3\,\text{mm}$)
- **`Layer_0_Registration_Crosshairs`:** 4-Corner alignment marks

**To ensure Cricut cuts the holes directly into the vinyl shapes:**
1. Click **Ungroup** at the top of the Layers panel to separate the groups.
2. Select the **Green Body** layer and the **Green Pill Slots**, then click the **Attach** (paperclip icon) at the bottom of the Layers panel. *(This locks the holes to the green vinyl so the blade cuts them out of the green sheet in their exact spots).*
3. Select the **Pink Features** (hair/wings/spines) and the **Pink Pill Slots**, then click **Attach**.

---

### 🎯 Pro-Tip: How the 4-Corner Alignment Markers Work (Registration Marks)

The 4 crosshairs in the corners (`Layer_0_Registration_Crosshairs`) are optional but give you **sub-millimeter layering precision**:

#### 1. Does the Cricut Machine Scan Them?
- **No optical scanning is needed.** The Cricut blade physically cuts 4 tiny crosshairs into the corners of each vinyl sheet if you attach them to your layers.

#### 2. The 2 Big Benefits:
- **Prevents Cricut Scrambling Pieces:** If you attach the 4 corner marks to the Pink layer AND to the Green layer, Cricut will not try to pack the pink pieces into the top corner to save material. It preserves the exact spacing between the pink hair up top and the pink tail spines at the bottom!
- **Zero-Guesswork Heat Pressing:**
  1. When pressing the **Green Body**, place a tiny piece of blue painter's tape or heat-resistant tape under each of the 4 corner crosshairs on the vest. Press for 10s. The green crosshairs will stick to the tape (not the shirt).
  2. When pressing the **Pink Accents**, simply look through the clear carrier sheet and line up the **pink crosshairs directly on top of the green crosshairs on the tape**.
  3. All hair, wings, and spines will snap into perfect anatomical alignment. Peel off the tape and crosshairs when finished!

*(Note: If your brother prefers to eyeball the alignment naturally, simply click the **eye icon** next to `Layer_0_Registration_Crosshairs` in Cricut Design Space to hide them before clicking Make It).*

---

### Step 4: Click "Make It" & Mirror for HTV / Iron-On
1. Click the green **Make It** button in the top-right corner.
2. Cricut will automatically sort the project onto **2 Cutting Mats**:
   - **Mat 1 (Green):** Green Body with its pre-aligned holes.
   - **Mat 2 (Pink):** Pink Hair, Wings, and Spines with their pre-aligned holes.
3. ⚠️ **CRITICAL HTV RULE:** On the left mat preview screen, **turn the "Mirror" toggle ON for EVERY mat!** 
   *(Heat transfer vinyl is always cut face-down through the adhesive side, so mirroring ensures it faces the correct way when ironed onto the shirt).*

---

### Step 5: Cut and Weed
1. Place the **Green HTV** onto your green Cricut grip mat, **SHINY CLEAR PLASTIC CARRIER SHEET DOWN** (matte vinyl facing UP).
2. Select **Everyday Iron-On** or **SportFlex Iron-On** as your material in Design Space.
3. Load the mat into the Cricut and press the flashing **Go (Cricut Logo)** button.
4. When finished, unload the mat and repeat for the **Pink HTV** mat.
5. **Weed the Vinyl:** Use your weeding tool or tweezers to peel away the excess background vinyl **and pop out the tiny 6×3mm pill slots**. You will be left with the clean vinyl shapes on the clear carrier sheet, with the pill slots completely cut out!

---

### Step 6: Iron / Heat Press onto the Mesh Vest
1. Lay the mesh pinnie vest flat on an ironing board or heat press mat.
2. **First Press (Green Base):**
   - Place the green body on the chest (approx. 2" below the neckline).
   - Cover with parchment paper or a Teflon sheet.
   - Press with an EasyPress or home iron at **305°F–315°F (150°C–155°C)** for **10–12 seconds** with medium pressure.
   - Let cool slightly (warm peel) and peel off the clear plastic carrier sheet.
3. **Second Press (Pink Accents):**
   - Lay the pink hair crest, wings, and tail spines over the green body (they overlap naturally in their exact registration spots).
   - Cover with parchment paper and press for **10–12 seconds**.
   - Peel off the clear plastic carrier sheet.
4. **Final Cure Press:** Cover the entire completed graphic with parchment paper and press firmly for **15 seconds** to permanently fuse the vinyl into the polyester mesh.

---

## 🧵 Step 7: Weaving the 100-LED Fairy Light Strand
Once the vinyl is ironed on:
1. **Start from the Inside:** Pass the LED strand starting near the lower-left hem / waist where the battery pack will sit.
2. **Weave Each LED:** 
   - Push the resin pebble LED from **inside the shirt ➔ through the mesh & vinyl pill slot ➔ to the outside**.
   - Lay the LED flat in the slot.
   - Push the wire **back into the next hole ➔ to the inside of the shirt** to travel behind the vest to the next position.
3. **Result:** All 100 LEDs shine brightly on the outside of the shirt with zero visible wire clutter on the front, and the flexible mesh pinnie remains lightweight, breathable, and comfortable for the 10K run!
